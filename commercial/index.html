Safe Installation

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

Safety is part of every commercial job we do. We inspect venting and draft, verify adequate combustion air, check relief valves and their discharge lines, verify expansion tanks are charged to the right pressure, and set mixing valves so water can be stored hot enough to control bacteria while reaching the tap at a safe temperature. Permits and inspections are handled as part of the job. You receive a written report that ranks each issue by priority and recommends a specific fix.

Regular professional service protects your people and your property, and acting on expert recommendations keeps small issues from becoming emergencies. It also creates a record. We keep service logs, temperature records, and a binder of reports for every account, with more frequent visits for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and mechanical rooms. When a landlord, insurer, or inspector asks for proof of due diligence, it's ready.

Emergency Service

After-Hours Commercial Water Heater Response

A hot water failure on a commercial property can shut down a kitchen, cancel a day of salon appointments, leave hotel guests with cold showers, and expose the owner to tenant disputes. Wauwatosa Water Heater provides fast emergency water heater service for commercial properties throughout Wauwatosa and surrounding areas, and we schedule planned work after hours or before opening to limit downtime.

Our technicians are equipped to handle the full range of commercial water heater emergencies, including gas valve failures, burned-out elements, ignition and flame sensor faults, tripped high-limit switches, leaking tanks, recirculation pumps that stopped, and tankless units stuck in lockout. We prioritize diagnosing the fault and restoring hot water as quickly as possible, isolating a failed unit so the rest of the system keeps running, then put maintenance in place to keep it from happening again. Every commercial response includes thorough documentation for your service records.

Preventive Care

Proactive Water Heater Care for Businesses
