Getting More Years From Your Water Heater in Waukesha

Many early failures start with maintenance that never happened, and the habits that prevent them are simple. Drain and flush the tank once a year to clear sediment, get the anode rod checked every few years, and test the relief valve each year so it doesn't seize. Set the thermostat close to 120 degrees for safer water and less scale. For a tankless heater in a hard-water area, plan on descaling once a year so the heat exchanger stays clean.

Age matters too. Most tank water heaters run 8 to 12 years, tankless units often reach 20 with regular descaling, and heat pump models typically run 10 to 15. If your heater is past that window and showing symptoms like rusty hot water, popping and rumbling, moisture around the base, or showers that go cold sooner, a planned replacement is almost always cheaper and far less stressful than an emergency one with water on the floor.

The earlier you report a hot water problem, the cheaper it usually is. A tank that's just begun to rumble can often be fixed with a flush, yet that same buildup, ignored for a few years, can overheat the bottom of the tank and burn out the lower element on an electric model, turning a simple visit into a replacement. Save our number in your contacts now so help is one call away the moment something sounds or looks wrong. Early intervention saves time, money, and cold showers.
